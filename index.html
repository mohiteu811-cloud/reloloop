01 — The Problem

People relocating in NZ are stuck. Their stuff is worth too much to leave, and too little to move.

$1,000+

Minimum charge to move a single item between NZ cities — more than the item itself is often worth.

Thousands

Of small-item enquiries turned away by relocation companies every year, with no good alternative.

Zero

Affordable solutions currently exist in the NZ market between full-truck moves and sketchy marketplaces.

The compounding pressure

Long-distance and export move enquiries have been declining for years. ReloLoop opens an entirely new revenue category — local circular moves — bringing in customers who would never have engaged with a relocation company in the first place.

The opening

A real white space between house moves and Marketplace.

Traditional relocation companies cost too much for a single fridge. Trade Me and Facebook Marketplace are full of flakes, no-shows, and condition surprises.

ReloLoop sits in the middle: trusted, professionally delivered, and priced for the way people actually move.

02 — How It Works

Four steps. Two cities. One loop.

A circular economy marketplace built specifically for people relocating within New Zealand — between cities or locally within the same city.

01

List before you leave

Seller lists item with photo and price before moving. Takes minutes.

02

Buyer browses ahead

Buyer in same city — or another Crown city — finds the item and purchases.

03

Crown delivers it

Crown or local partner moves the item, scheduled into existing daily runs. Within 5 business days.

04

Everyone wins

Seller gets cash. Buyer gets furniture. Crown gets new revenue. Landfill stays empty.

03 — The Crown Partnership

Not a competitor. A complement.

Crown's expertise is premium full-household moves and high-value items. ReloLoop handles the market Crown cannot cost-effectively serve today — the small-item enquiries that already arrive every day and currently get turned away.

Crown brings

Decades of trust. Real delivery capability in every major NZ city. An established customer base. Insurance frameworks already in place. ReloLoop doesn't need to build from scratch — it plugs into something that already works.

ReloLoop brings

The missing piece. A platform that catches enquiries Crown currently turns away, turns them into actual transactions, and sends delivery work back to Crown. Plus a sustainability story corporates and councils are actively looking for.

Operational Promise

Each booking capped at 2–3 items. Deliveries scheduled into Crown's existing daily runs within 5 business days. Zero disruption to core operations. Full household moves stay exclusively within Crown's premium service.

05 — Circular Economy

Not a marketplace. An environmental initiative.

  • Zero unnecessary freight

    Items never leave the city — sold locally, delivered locally as part of existing routes.

  • Reduces landfill waste

    Usable goods find new owners instead of being discarded between moves.

  • Grant eligible

    NZ Government and councils actively fund circular economy initiatives. MBIE-aligned.

  • ESG aligned

    Strengthens Crown's sustainability credentials with corporate clients increasingly driven by ESG mandates.

The North Star
10× growth.
Platinum status.

If Crown captures the freight CO₂ saved through ReloLoop, EcoVadis Platinum becomes within reach. Replicated across markets, this takes Crown from a $1–2B company to a $10B company in five years.

06 — Strategic Growth Layers

Five layers. Compounding moats.

Layer 01

Corporate moves (Mobility Team)

Crown already works with corporates for international moves. Extend ReloLoop as a perk: "Move to Wellington? Sell your furniture in Auckland via ReloLoop — Crown handles end-to-end." That cross-market hand-off creates B2B recurring referrals.

High Conviction · Near-term
Layer 02

"Crown Certified" used goods

Secondhand marketplaces are trust-deficit environments. Crown's brand becomes a quality signal: "Crown-Inspected & Guaranteed Delivery" — cleaned, sanitised, professionally moved. Customers pay extra for reliability — instant margin.

High-margin Opportunity
Layer 03

Item bundles & room packs

Aggregate ReloLoop listings into "studio set" or "family starter pack" bundles delivered together. Faster sales velocity, higher ticket size, less logistics fragmentation per trip.

Pilot: AKL ↔ WELL ↔ CHC
Layer 04

Sustainability grants

NZ government loves circular economy pilots. Pitch for council or MBIE funding — could offset early operational costs or marketing entirely. Bonus revenue, not core.

Bonus, Not Revenue Line
Layer 05

Data & analytics

Once volume builds, sell anonymised relocation and consumption data — routes, demand clusters, item categories — to furniture retailers, appliance brands, and real estate developers.

Instrument Now · Monetise Later
The Honest Risk

Marketplace liquidity

Chicken-and-egg: needing buyers and sellers in the same cities at the same time. The AKL–WELL–CHC pilot focus is the right call to solve this — concentrated demand corridors first, then expand.

Acknowledged · Mitigated
